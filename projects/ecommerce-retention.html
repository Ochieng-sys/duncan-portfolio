E-Commerce Customer Retention Analysis

An end-to-end customer intelligence pipeline combining Python ETL, machine learning, relational data modeling, and Power BI to analyze retention and revenue risk.

Python MySQL Power BI DAX Machine Learning

01 — THE BUSINESS PROBLEM

Understanding customer retention and revenue risk.

Customer retention is a critical component of sustainable e-commerce growth. This project was designed to analyze customer behavior and identify patterns associated with churn and customer lifetime value.

The objective was to build a complete analytical pipeline rather than treating the dashboard as an isolated reporting layer.

02 — THE PIPELINE

From raw customer data to retention intelligence.

01

Python ETL

Prepared and transformed customer and transactional data for analysis.

02

MySQL

Organized analytical data using a relational star-schema architecture.

03

Churn Modeling

Applied machine-learning techniques to identify customer churn risk.

04

Power BI

Developed an executive dashboard using DAX-driven metrics.

03 — ANALYTICAL FOCUS

Connecting customer behavior with business value.

01

Customer Retention

Analyzed customer behavior to identify patterns associated with retention and potential churn.

02
